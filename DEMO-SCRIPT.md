# SortSmart demo video script

Total length: about 1 minute 38 seconds. The video file is
`demo/sortsmart-demo.webm` (1280x800). Record this voiceover over it, or
read it while recording your own screen walkthrough.

## 0:00 to 0:10, title

"Hello, I am Ibrahim Yahaya from Bida, Niger State, Nigeria, a student at
the Federal University of Technology Minna. This is SortSmart, a waste
sorting helper for Nigerian households. I built it for the Arbiter Hacks
Theme Track."

## 0:10 to 0:22, look it up

"In most homes here, everything goes into one bag: plastic bottles, yam
peels, old batteries, sachet nylon. SortSmart has 54 common household items.
Type any item and it tells you where it goes."

## 0:22 to 0:32, item detail

"Pick an item and you get the category, what to do with it, and why. An old
battery is hazardous, so the app tells you to keep it sealed and take it to a
collection point, not the normal bin."

## 0:32 to 0:42, sorting quiz

"There is a short quiz to teach sorting. Eight questions, which bin does this
go in, with an explanation after every answer."

## 0:42 to 0:50, score

"You get a score at the end, so you can see what you got wrong and try again."

## 0:50 to 1:08, the app working live

"Here is the app working live. Search, filter by category, answer quiz
questions, and log items, all in one place. It runs in the browser with no
account and no backend."

## 1:08 to 1:20, waste log

"You can log what you throw away. The log shows your totals by category, with
percentages, and a chart of the last seven days. Entries are saved on your
device."

## 1:20 to 1:32, guide

"The guide explains where recyclables actually go in Nigeria, buy-back
centres and waste collectors, what never to burn or dump, and how to start
composting at home."

## 1:32 to 1:38, closing

"SortSmart. Know where your waste goes. Thank you."

## Notes for recording

- The app must be submitted with a public repo link and this video uploaded
  to YouTube or Vimeo.
- If you record your own walkthrough instead, follow the same order:
  title, lookup, one item detail, quiz, score, log, guide, closing.
- Keep it under 3 minutes. The hackathon limit is 2 to 3 minutes max.
